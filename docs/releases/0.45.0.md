# Tohyee 0.45.0

Everything merged since 0.44.0.

## Before you update

- **Database changes:** one for each organisation (0308, late fees: settings, customers never charged, and what's been charged). Nothing existing is changed. Back up first, as for any update.
- **Windows servers on 0.44.0** can use **Update now** in Tohyee (a server admin's menu) for this update.
- **Not yet reviewed by the owner:** late fees (LATE1-LATE12), written up in `docs/ACCOUNTING-EXAMPLES.md`. Not yet tested with an invoice in another currency.

## New

- **Late fees** (Sales › Late fees): an option an admin turns on, after checking their terms of trade. New Zealand law doesn't add late fees or interest by itself.
  - A flat fee, once per overdue invoice, with GST like the sale it's on.
  - Simple interest at a yearly rate on what's still owing each day, exempt from GST. Part payments count from their date.
  - Days of grace, a minimum owing, and customers who are never charged.
  - Each overdue invoice gets a late payment invoice of its own; the overdue invoice isn't changed.
  - See what's due first and press a button, or have it charge by itself on the 1st of each month. Drafts to check, or approved straight away.
