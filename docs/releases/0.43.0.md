# Tohyee 0.43.0

Everything merged since 0.42.0.

## Before you update

- **Database changes:** four for each organisation (0303 to 0306). They add columns for a shop order's region and for tracking on Spreading items, and tables for saved searches. Nothing existing is changed. Back up first, as for any update.
- **Not yet reviewed by the owner:** the Spreading rules added here follow common New Zealand practice and are written up as examples SW24 and SW25 in `docs/ACCOUNTING-EXAMPLES.md`, with SHP1 to SHP5 for shop orders' regions.
- **Emails from saved searches** need the server's email settings filled in. Without them nothing is sent. Turn the job off altogether with `TOHYEE_SAVED_SEARCH_EMAILS=off`.

## New

- **Saved searches** (Reports › Detail): a list of one kind of record, filtered, with the columns and sort you choose, kept under a name. It always shows what's in the books now.
  - Invoices, bills, ledger lines, money spent and received, contacts and items.
  - Filter on text, amounts, dates (including moving ranges such as this month or the last 30 days) and choices such as status. Invoices and bills have Still owing and Days overdue.
  - Your own custom fields and tracking categories are fields too, along with the customer's or supplier's own fields on an invoice, bill or bank transaction.
  - **Calculated columns:** up to five per search, each a sum over the record's number fields, such as `{total} - {owing}`.
  - A count of everything that matches, a total for each money column, and a CSV download of up to 5,000 rows.
  - A search is yours until a bookkeeper or above shares it.
  - **Show on Home** puts a search on Home with how many match and its first total.
  - **Email me this** sends it to you every day, week or month: every time, only when there's something in it, or only when there's something new.
  - **Save this view** on Invoices and Bills starts a search from the tab, search text and sort you're looking at.
- **Spreading:**
  - Tracking and custom fields on each item, carried onto the journals it posts. A required one that's missing stops the month and names the item.
  - When a tab is out against its ledger account, it lists what's behind the difference.
  - AI tools to read the worksheet, add items and post a month. Undoing a month and removing an item show what they'll do first and wait for a yes.
- **Shopify:** each order keeps its region, town and country, so Analytics can map sales.

## Fixed

- **Analytics:** a chart that can't be drawn yet says what's missing instead of blanking the whole report.
