# Tohyee 0.46.0

Everything merged since 0.45.0.

## Before you update

- **Database changes:** one for the server (0031, whether a login's email address has been confirmed) and nine for each organisation (0309 to 0318, with no 0312): Spreading suggestions, sales platform fixes, farm stock and safety emails, tax rates and decisions, cancelled Shopify shipments, stock moves between farms, water pipes and task categories. Nothing existing is removed. Back up first, as for any update.
- **Unpaid leave:** leave of more than a week that doesn't count now moves the anniversary only by the days over one week. Leave already recorded keeps the old move until someone presses "Use the current rule" on it.
- **Releases aren't signed yet.** Windows servers can now refuse an update the owner didn't sign, but no signing key has been added, so this release says "not signed" and is still checked by its SHA-256. See `docs/OWNER-SETUP-SIGNING.md`.
- **Not yet tried on a real Windows computer:** Update now, and the new failed-update recovery. If an update doesn't come back, run TohyeeSetup-0.46.0.exe on the server computer.
- **Not yet reviewed by the owner or anyone qualified:** the farm water sums (FW1-FW13: an estimate, not a hydraulic design), and the rules marked "not yet reviewed" for the review fixes (BKF, TXF, SPF, OPF, LATE12-14, SW26-SW30, FT9) in `docs/ACCOUNTING-EXAMPLES.md`.

## New

- **Farm water** (Farm › Water). A guide for choosing pipe, not a design.
  - **Size a pipe:** type a flow, a length and a rise. Each polyethylene size shows the water's speed, the friction and the pressure needed, and the smallest size at or under 1.5 m/s is suggested.
  - **How much the stock drink:** head counts by class become litres a day and the flow needed at the peak. One button fills the head in from the farm's mobs, as a best guess that can be changed.
  - **Match a pump:** type points from a pump's chart and a pipe, and it finds the flow the pump would deliver, or says the pump can't do it.
  - **The farm's mapped pipes:** each with its length from the map and, once it has a size and a flow, what it does. The metres of each pipe size are added up.
  - 10% is added to pipe friction for bends and fittings unless changed. Left out: ring mains, fittings counted one by one, the rise worked out from the land, suction lift.
- **Move stock to another farm:** a mob, or part of one with its tagged animals, moves between two farms in the same organisation as a move, not a sale. Withholding dates go with the animals.
- **Farm tasks: categories.** A task can have a category in the farm's own words, and the list can be narrowed to one.
- **Spreading:**
  - **Suggestions** for bills and invoices that look like they cover more than one month, to add or dismiss.
  - **Change** an item that's running: move its last day, or change what's still to come on an accrual.
- **Provisional tax options:** standard, estimation, ratio, or AIM through other software, for each income year. The instalments, due dates and interest estimate follow the option.
- **Trusts:** lines trusts (33%) and legacy superannuation funds (28%). A beneficiary's income can be marked as all from a Māori authority distribution.
- **Shopify:** a shipment cancelled after Sales when sent counted it is reversed, with stock back at the cost it went out at.
- **Error pages** with plain words, Try again and a way back. A screen keeps its last good data when a refresh fails.
- **Safety:** a report that may need notifying emails the owners and admins, and stays first on Today until a WorkSafe notice is recorded.

## Fixed

- **Bank and ledger:** a possible duplicate statement line gets no one-click match; paying an invoice for more than it owes is refused unless the extra is kept as credit; Remove & redo also voids an adjustment the line posted; lines in a locked period can't be excluded or included; Edit this journal is for manual journals only; whole-yen currencies round properly on credits and refunds.
- **Late fees:** the fee is converted into the invoice's currency; turning the monthly run on part way through a month waits for the next 1st.
- **Tax and payroll:** a deceased estate's last year at 33%; a 0% depreciation rate, so a new commercial building can get Investment Boost; IR4 imputation boxes; no KiwiSaver employer minimum for members under 16 or 65 and over; clock-out times compared properly in any time zone.
- **Sales platforms and stock:** stock on orders follows Allow negative stock; a payout whose deposit was already entered by hand is linked, not posted twice; a full re-read of orders carries on past 200; WooCommerce prices that include GST are no longer refused; one order that can't be read no longer stops the sync.
- **Farm and operations:** job visit times in any time zone; medicine stock in units that can't be counted is refused; treatments logged from Log take stock off; an expired quote can't be sent for signing or signed.
- **Phones:** 30 tables now scroll inside their box, and save errors scroll into view.
- **Windows updates:** a failed update puts the old program back; Update now works more than once; a release becomes "latest" only when all its files are there.

## Security

- A login set up from a hand-passed setup link is marked not confirmed, and another organisation's invitation to it waits for an emailed link.
- Server admins can't sign in with Google, so they always use two-step sign-in.
- Restores work from a private copy of the backup file.
- Wrong current passwords count towards the lockout when changing a password.
- Bank file feeds read statement files only from senders the receiving mail service confirmed.
- Organisations' database sources and post boxes are checked against a server-admin setting.
- Windows servers check an update's signature once the owner adds a key.

## For AI keys

- New look-only tools: `get_farm_water`, `size_farm_pipe`, `stock_water_demand`, `match_farm_pump`, `get_spreading_suggestions`.
- New tools that change things: `move_mob_to_farm`, `change_spreading_item`, `dismiss_spreading_suggestion`. `add_farm_task` takes a category.
