# Tohyee 0.40.0

Everything merged since 0.39.0. 0.39.0 wasn't published on its own, so updating from 0.38.0 to 0.40.0 brings in [0.39.0's changes](0.39.0.md) too.

## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts.** This release adds tenant migrations:
  - 0295: business structure;
  - 0296 and 0297: income tax for trusts;
  - 0298: the medicine cabinet;
  - 0299: the fuel excise refund.
- **Not yet reviewed:** trust tax (TRU1–TRU27), the business structure (ENT1–ENT8), the medicine cabinet (MED1–MED10) and the fuel excise refund (FUEL1–FUEL12) follow common practice and haven't been reviewed by an accountant.
- **Check before you rely on it:**
  - Trust tax credits are shared with beneficiaries in proportion to income.
  - Tax the trust pays for a beneficiary is their share of tax on all their income.
  - The fuel refund suggests coding the money that comes back to fuel with GST at 15%.

## New

- **Business structure:** Settings › Organisation records whether you're a company, sole trader, partnership, trust and so on, and your IRD number (checked). Income tax, draft statements and payroll follow it.
- **Income tax for trusts:**
  - Beneficiaries, and the 33% or 39% trustee rate.
  - The minor and corporate beneficiary rules.
  - Deceased estates and disabled beneficiary trusts.
  - Credits shared with beneficiaries, and the trust paying a beneficiary's tax.
  - Distributions and beneficiary current accounts.
  - Complying, foreign and non-complying trusts (45% on taxable distributions).
  - The IR6 and an IR6B for each beneficiary, with the 2025 or 2026 form's box numbers.
- **Medicine cabinet (farms):**
  - Each farm's animal medicines on a vending-machine shelf, with what's on hand, running low, out and expiring.
  - Starter lists for dairy and sheep farms.
  - Treatments take stock off. "Used" covers teat spray and the footbath.
  - Move stock between farms, set reorder levels, or take a photo of your own product.
  - Detailed, unbranded drawings of each kind of pack.
- **Fuel excise refund (farms):**
  - The quarter's petrol, read from your bills and spend money, kept separate from diesel.
  - The petrol split between your quads, bikes and machines with sliders, and the bulk tank.
  - The refund at 80.5276c a litre for NZTA's MR70, with the due date.
  - Three simple steps on a phone.
- **Website:** redesigned in blue, with plainer wording and light and dark modes.
