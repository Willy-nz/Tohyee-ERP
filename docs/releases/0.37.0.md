# Tohyee 0.37.0

Everything merged since 0.36.0. **Tohyee has moved to this repository,** and this release looks here for its updates.

## Before you update

- **Updates now come from Willy-nz/Tohyee-ERP.** After this update, Tohyee, the Windows tray app, the Android app link and the camera post box installer all look in the new repository. Nothing else changes.
- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts.** This release adds tenant migrations 0287 to 0289. Released migrations are now one file each and checked against their released checksums.
- **Not yet reviewed:** the Google Merchant Center, Business Profile and Ads examples (GMC, GBP, GADS), Shopify's archived orders (SWS15) and the Analytics dropdown on a profit and loss (APS1–APS10) follow common practice.
- **Not tried for real:** Google Business Profile and Google Ads need Google to approve the Google Cloud project before they load; until then a load explains Google's refusal.

## New

- **Analytics text boxes and headings are changed beside the report:** the report stays in view, each change shows as a draft, with labelled buttons for bold, italic, links, lists, size, alignment and text colour. Click a chart's heading to change its words and colour. Save, Cancel, and a question before leaving with unsaved text.
- **A profit and loss on an Analytics page follows the page's dropdown:** choose a tracking value (Region, Location or your own category) or a salesperson, and a draft profit and loss shows just that.
- **Download PDF on a report's print page:** the PDF keeps the report's orientation. Printing fits one sheet a page where it can and never cuts a tile.
- **Analytics data sources:** Google Merchant Center, Google Business Profile and Google Ads.
- **Save as draft and Publish on every standard report;** Edit layout keeps the report's dates and comparisons.
- **AI:** add pictures to Analytics reports, delete an Analytics report at Full access after a preview, and publish custom reports.
- Illustrated icons on Analytics › Connect your data.

## Fixed

- **Shopify:** archived orders leave To send, and listed orders are read again on each sync.
