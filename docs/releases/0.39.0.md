# Tohyee 0.39.0

Everything merged since 0.38.0.

## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts.** This release adds tenant migrations 0292 (farm chat), 0293 (voice note lengths) and 0294 (team chat, off until an admin turns it on).
- **Not yet reviewed:** farm chat (FCH1–FCH11) and team chat (TCH1–TCH8) follow common practice.
- **Not tried for real:** voice notes haven't been recorded or played on a real phone yet, nor the Android app's microphone. A note recorded on one kind of phone may not play on another; tell us if one doesn't.
- **Recording in a browser needs https.** Browsers only allow the microphone on a secure address. The Android app always uses https.

## New

- **Farm chat:** each farm has a Chat tab for everyone who can see the farm, with an unread count. Messages can have photos and say which paddocks, mobs or tasks they're about; a paddock's or mob's timeline shows the messages about it.
- **Voice notes:** record up to 3 minutes on the Chat screen and play it back before sending. The Android app asks for the microphone the first time.
- **Chat for the whole organisation:** a Chat app for organisations, farm or not, turned on under Settings › Modules. One chat for everyone but farm staff and report viewers; viewers read only. Words, photos and voice notes, an unread dot on the app switcher, and messages kept on your own server.
- **AI and chat:** your AI can read a farm's chat or the team chat and post in it when you ask, marked "via their AI". It can see that a voice note is there, but can't hear it.
