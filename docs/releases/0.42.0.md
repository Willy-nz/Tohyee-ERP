# Tohyee 0.42.0

Everything merged since 0.41.0.

## Before you update

- **Database changes:** three for each organisation (0300 to 0302), all adding tables and columns for Spreading. Nothing existing is changed. Back up first, as for any update.
- **Not yet reviewed by the owner:** the accounting rules behind Spreading follow common New Zealand practice and are written up as examples SW1 to SW23 in `docs/ACCOUNTING-EXAMPLES.md`. Check a month's preview against your own working before you rely on it.
- **Nothing happens until it's turned on.** An admin turns Spreading on under Accounting › Month end.

## New

- **Spreading** (Accounting › Month end): one worksheet in place of the spreadsheet for prepayments, income in advance and accruals.
  - Four tabs: Paid in advance, Income in advance, Not billed yet, Not invoiced yet. Each has its own balance sheet account.
  - Add something once and say how to spread it: evenly by month, by days, all in one month, your own amounts, or when it's done.
  - A grid with a row for each item and a column for each month.
  - **Month end** shows what a month will post, then posts one journal for the kind, dated the month's last day. Nothing posts by itself. Months go in order, a locked month is refused, and the latest month can be undone.
  - Each tab says whether it matches its account in the ledger, or how much it's out by.
  - **Spread this** on an approved bill or invoice, and **Spread** on a spend or receive money transaction: the line goes on the worksheet without being recoded. GST isn't touched. Tracking carries through.
  - A bill, invoice or bank line can be marked as the one an accrual was waiting for. What it came to over or under goes in that month.
  - Something added late has the months already posted caught up in the next month.
  - Shop orders paid and not sent yet (Sales when sent) show beside Income in advance and are counted in the check.
  - **Books kept in other software:** the worksheet can run on its own, with each month's journal downloaded as a CSV file. Items can be brought in from a CSV file, and the worksheet downloaded as one.
- **Invoices and Bills:** click a column heading to sort, and each tab shows how many it holds.
- **CRM:** leads and win rate as bars over time, and a chooser for which columns show on Leads.
