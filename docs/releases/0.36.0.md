Everything merged since 0.35.0.

## Before you update

- **The database updates itself when Tohyee starts.** This release adds core migrations 0026 to 0029 and tenant migrations 0282 to 0286.
- **AI keys can now stop on their own:** a key not used for 90 days stops working, and a key can have an end date. Its owner is warned a week before, in Tohyee and by email. Check your AI keys after updating.
- **Typed queries on database sources can only call functions Tohyee knows.** A saved source whose query calls one of the database's own functions stops refreshing, with a message naming the function, until an owner or admin lists it on the connection under Extra functions.
- **The older AI dashboards have gone from the AI's tools.** Reports are made in Analytics; the AI workspace has "Move to Analytics" for the old ones.
- **The balance sheet shows fixed assets net of accumulated depreciation**, and financial reports show account names without the code.
- **Not yet reviewed:** the rounding examples (RND1–RND7), more than one draft set of annual statements (FS16–FS19) and the details of the AI and Analytics changes below are Claude's, following common practice where it has an answer.
- **Not tried for real:** the PowerPoint add-in inside PowerPoint, and the database login warning and the known function lists on MySQL and SQL Server.

## New

- **Analytics reports on paper:**
  - print or save as PDF from the browser, one page per A4 sheet, portrait or landscape; the paper settings are kept with the report;
  - a cover, a contents sheet (which can list each page's tiles) and a running header;
  - long pages and long writing carry on over more sheets, keeping headings with their rows and repeating column headings;
  - a sign-off item, and a directory item filled from the organisation's settings.
- **The organisation's directory** in Settings: registered office, numbers, directors or trustees, and advisers. The AI and the PowerPoint add-in can read it.
- **Rounded report layouts that always add up:** whole dollars, thousands or millions on the profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flows, changes in equity and the year-end statements. Totals are the sum of the rounded lines.
- **Analytics:**
  - present a report as slides, with a background picture for a page;
  - put a draft or published accounting report on a page;
  - commentary boxes the AI writes and a person accepts;
  - tidier defaults: no cents on big key figures, shares as percentages;
  - a failed refresh says why, has Try again, and owners and admins are emailed when a source stops refreshing;
  - testing a database connection warns when its login can do more than read.
- **The AI and Analytics:** it can set a report's theme, pages and slicers, take tiles off, and has a format guide and report templates.
- **The AI on the farm:**
  - farm staff can use an AI for field work on their own farms;
  - it can record readings, grazings, feed, rain, tasks, treatments, body condition, weights, soil samples, breeding, effluent pond levels and effluent applications. The ponding check on an application is what a person saw, never made up;
  - at Make and post it can move and count a mob, record births and deaths in it, and record livestock movements in the books' head count; sell, buy, change class, split and merge mobs; and at Full access, after a preview, undo a mob's event or void a livestock movement;
  - one tool answers "what's due on the farm?": tasks, effluent dates, safety items, and stock and paddocks still under a withholding;
  - many more farm screens can be read: Home cards, calendar, rotation, tractor, effluent, hazards, co-op shares, year figures, valuation methods, single paddocks, mobs and tractor jobs, tasks due and the weather;
  - the Livestock year-end screens can be read too: openings, rates, high-priced stock, account sales, management values and report, and the ageing and valuation previews;
  - health and safety can be read by those who can already see it (restricted report details stay restricted), and the security cameras' list and events log, never pictures or video;
  - it can see who's sharing where they are with a farm, as the farm map shows that farm's members: only people who chose to share, and only their latest position;
  - every farm and livestock screen now has a tool for the AI to read it, or is left to the screen with a reason (files and pictures, sign-ins, and set-up for admins).
- **AI connections:**
  - a key that stopped by itself has Make a new one, which fills the form with its name and choices;
  - a key or connected app can be Reports only;
  - the AI page shows the address a key was last used from, and a Full access key's owner is emailed when it's used from a new address;
  - an owner can have a person approve voids, deletes and roll-backs;
  - text from outside the organisation (emailed bills, bank lines, shop orders) is marked, and the AI is told it's never an instruction.
- **Annual financial statements:** more than one draft set a year, in Reports › Drafts.
- **Report layout editor:** drag accounts between groups; move a line between groups in one step on cash flows and changes in equity.
- **PowerPoint add-in:** a page anyone can open to download the add-in and see how to add it to PowerPoint. Settings › PowerPoint add-in has its address to send to people.

## Fixed

## Security

- Typed database queries can only call known functions, plus the ones a connection allows.
- The Analytics read-only query check refuses functions that run a query held in text, and quoted function names.
- Plain database error messages, a ceiling on password hash cost, weekly dependency updates, and GitHub Actions pinned to exact commits.
